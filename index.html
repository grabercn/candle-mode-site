Questions

Good questions.

Does Candle Mode track my location?

Only on your phone. Location is checked on-device to know when you've arrived somewhere, and it's never uploaded or shared.

Which apps can it lock?

Any app you choose. Most people start with TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. Calls, texts and maps are never blocked.

Is it iPhone only?

iPhone first, using Apple's Screen Time system so it's private and reliable. Android may come later.

What if there's an emergency?

Calls and texts always work, and you can unlock anytime after a short pause.
